# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Principal investigators, lab managers and recruiters opening Sahar Rao's portfolio link between meetings, usually on a laptop in a bright office. They decide in under a minute whether this student reads data carefully and builds clean, accessible interfaces for it.

## Product Purpose
A case study, originally a class assignment: Intel's public 2030/2040/2050 sustainability goals, set against Intel's own five years of reported data (2021–2025). Success is a reader leaving with one accurate status per goal and trusting the page because every number is sourced and every limit is stated.

## Brand Personality
Exact, industrial, candid. The physical object is a fab's cleanroom under amber photolithography light and the test report pinned beside it: plain numbers, honest gaps, no cheerleading.

## Anti-references
- Corporate sustainability microsites: stock leaves, gradient globes, "our journey" copy.
- The original version of this page: Intel-blue glass cards, orbit decorations, uppercase eyebrows over every section.
- SaaS dashboards: hero metrics, identical card grids.

## Design Principles
1. Every number traces to a page in Intel's report; computed figures say they were computed.
2. Show the gap, not the slogan: each goal ends in one plain status.
3. Time to scale: deadlines sit where they fall on a real axis.
4. The data table is the source; charts are drawn from it and the table stays readable without JavaScript.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA. Charts have their data as real tables. Full keyboard use, visible focus, reduced-motion alternatives, and no information carried by color alone.
